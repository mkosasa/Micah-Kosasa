**HOW TO WORK THE PULL REQUEST**

This same review is also waiting in your repository as a pull request, so you can read it next to your own file and reply in place. If you have not used one before, a pull request is just a proposed change to your repo held open for discussion — nothing in it touches your main branch until you decide it should.

Where to find it: open your repository on github.com and click the Pull requests tab at the top. It is titled "Stage 1.1 review — engagement brief." You will also have an email from GitHub.

How to work it, in the order that actually helps:

* Read it yourself first, before changing anything. Form your own view. Disagreeing with a point and saying why is a legitimate, senior response — it is not the same as ignoring it.

* Then stress-test it. Paste the review and your brief into your AI assistant and ask it to do two things: explain anything you are unsure about, and argue the other side — where might I be wrong, and what would you give up by making each change. You are building judgment here, not executing a punch list.

* Write the changes yourself. For a brief this matters more than usual: a hypothesis you did not generate cannot be honestly compared against your own model in Stage 3, and that comparison is the whole reason the brief is written first.

* Reply in the pull request thread with what you changed and what you pushed back on, then commit and push to your main branch as usual. Writing down the reasoning is exactly how this works on a real team.

* Close the pull request when you are done with it. You do not need to merge it — merging would add a copy of this review into your repo as a file, which you are welcome to keep if you find it useful, but closing it is the normal ending. The conversation stays visible either way.

One standing rule for this stage: do not revise your hypothesis to match what your model later tells you. If the model contradicts the brief, that is a finding, not an error. Stage 3 asks you to explain the gap, and a brief quietly edited to be right afterwards has nothing left to explain.
