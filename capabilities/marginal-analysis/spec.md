## Constraints

- Season: 36 weeks
- Fixed costs: $20,000
- Beds: 64 beds (16 beds × 4 plots)
- The farmer: $50,000 a season, half her time in the field — 720 field hours at an implied $34.72/hr
- Up to 4 temporary workers at $25,000 each, 1,440 hours each, $17.36/hr
